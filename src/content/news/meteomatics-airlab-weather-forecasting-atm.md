## A New Approach to Weather in ATM

Weather is one of the most significant factors affecting air traffic efficiency. Delays, reroutings, and capacity reductions caused by adverse weather conditions cost the aviation industry billions each year.

AIR Lab partnered with Meteomatics to explore how hyper-local weather forecasting could be integrated directly into ATM decision-making tools through the Regional Collaboration Platform (RCP).

## SING1K — Singapore at 1km Resolution

Together, we developed **SING1K**, a custom weather forecast model delivering data at **1×1 km resolution** across Singapore's airspace — a level of precision previously unavailable to air traffic controllers in the region.

SING1K provides:
- High-resolution precipitation forecasts
- Wind shear detection
- Turbulence probability mapping
- Integration with RCP live data feeds

## Impact

By integrating SING1K into the RCP, ANSPs can now access weather intelligence directly alongside traffic data — enabling better-informed decisions on routing, sequencing, and capacity management.

> "This collaboration marks a significant advancement in weather forecasting for ATM in Singapore, using advanced technology to reduce weather risks and improve air traffic safety."
> — Arjen Piest, Meteomatics

## What's Next

AIR Lab and Meteomatics continue to refine the model, exploring extensions to neighbouring FIRs across Southeast Asia as more ANSPs join the RCP.
