Air traffic management (ATM) is like a finely tuned machine where aircraft act as interlocking gears, and a wide ecosystem of stakeholders—air navigation service providers (ANSPs), regulators, airlines, and technology partners—must work in harmony to keep the system safe, efficient, and resilient.

The **Regional Collaboration Platform (RCP)** is a cloud-based sandbox designed to catalyze this kind of regional cooperation. It provides shared research tools that allow stakeholders to collaboratively **analyze, simulate, and optimize airspace operations**, with a strong focus on operational efficiency and sustainable aviation practices.

## Regional Collaboration Platform (RCP) Summary

The Regional Collaboration Platform (RCP) is a cloud-based, open-source sandbox environment that enables regional air traffic management (ATM) stakeholders—such as ANSPs, regulators, airlines, and technology partners—to collaboratively analyze, simulate, and optimize airspace operations. It is designed to support safe, efficient, and sustainable aviation across regions.

### Core Characteristics

The Regional Collaboration Platform (RCP) is a cloud-based experimentation and research environment designed to support regional Air Traffic Management (ATM) collaboration.

It integrates live, historical, and simulated data so that multiple stakeholders—Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs), regulators, airlines, research institutes, and industry partners—can jointly analyze, simulate, and optimize airspace operations at a regional scale.

- Act as a **sandbox for regional cooperation**, where cross-border ATM questions can be explored safely.
- Enable **joint experimentation** on operational efficiency, safety, capacity, and environmental performance.
- Support **sustainable aviation practices** by allowing stakeholders to test and evaluate greener operational concepts.

- **Cloud-based experimentation platform**: Accessible to multiple organizations, providing shared tools and data services.
- **Data integration**: Combines
  - Live operational data
  - Historical datasets
  - Simulated and synthetic scenarios
- **Open standards alignment**: Designed to interoperate with global ATM information frameworks, including:
  - **ICAO SWIM (System Wide Information Management)** for standardized information exchange
  - **FF-ICE (Flight and Flow Information for a Collaborative Environment)** for trajectory- and flow-centric information sharing

**Five Years of Regional Research**

Since 2019, AIR Lab has used the RCP to address regional ATM challenges involving safe information exchange, AI-based optimization, and environmental sustainability. The platform has matured into a shared environment where ANSPs, research institutions, and industry partners collaborate on problems that exceed the scope of any single organization.

**Why Regional Collaboration Matters**

While individual ANSPs can model their own airspace in detail, complex cross-border dynamics—such as new hubs, major weather events, and shifting traffic patterns—only become visible when the entire region is modeled together. The RCP exposes these interdependencies, enabling:

- Anticipation and management of cross-border impacts.
- Coordinated responses to regional disruptions.
- Co-design of more efficient and sustainable regional operations.
