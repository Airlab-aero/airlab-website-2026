### A Day in the Life of Sanjukta Saha at AIR Lab

Sanjukta Saha is part of the RCP Squad at AIR Lab — a joint initiative between Thales and CAAS. The lab focuses on innovative solutions in:

- **Trajectory Based Operations (TBO)**
- **Green Aviation**
- **Open Secure Platform**

All of these initiatives aim to improve **efficiency, sustainability, and safety** in Air Traffic Management.

Her day typically starts with **squad stand-ups**, where the team shares progress and discusses blockers. She frequently collaborates with **technical leads** and **solutions architects** on architectural decisions and emerging technologies.

A key part of her work involves **workshops with ATCOs (air traffic controllers)**. She finds these especially valuable because they deepen the team's understanding of real-world operational needs and the aviation domain.

---

### Skills for Aspiring Software Engineers

Sanjukta highlights several important skills and mindsets:

- **Cloud proficiency**: Comfort with cloud tools and platforms is essential as development increasingly becomes **cloud-native**.
- **Product-focused engineering**: Engineers should align technical decisions with **end-user needs** and real-world use cases.
- **Continuous learning**: She notes, *"The best engineers I work with are constantly upgrading their skills."* Staying current with tools, frameworks, and practices is critical.

---

### Trends to Watch

Sanjukta points to **generative AI** as a major emerging trend:

- Useful for **creating boilerplate code**
- Helpful for **learning new technologies** and exploring unfamiliar stacks

She is particularly curious about how generative AI will **influence and transform the aviation sector**, from development workflows to operational decision support.
